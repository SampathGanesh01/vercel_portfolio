## Overview

ORo is a consumer robot built by Ogmen Robotics, designed to provide autonomous home-based dog care. As the primary Product Owner and Robotics Architect, I drove the end-to-end development of the platform — from initial product vision through architecture, team coordination, and deployment.

The product required tight integration across Embedded Systems, Robotics Software, Computer Vision, and AI/ML, making cross-functional alignment and clear product ownership critical to delivering a reliable, shippable robot.

## My Role

I owned the product roadmap and acted as the bridge between business goals and engineering execution. This meant:

- Defining the product vision, feature priorities, and success metrics for each release cycle
- Running Agile/Scrum ceremonies (sprint planning, backlog grooming, retrospectives) for a team of 8 engineers tracked in Jira
- Making trade-off decisions between autonomy reliability, hardware constraints, and user experience

## Autonomous Navigation

The core of ORo's value is its ability to navigate a home environment reliably without human intervention. I architected and oversaw the deployment of:

- **ROS 2 + Nav2** for full autonomous indoor navigation with dynamic obstacle avoidance
- **SLAM** (2D Lidar and Visual) for accurate mapping and real-time localization
- **BehaviorTree.CPP** for modular task sequencing — managing states like treat dispensing, interactive play, and returning to dock

## AI-Powered Interaction

To make ORo feel intuitive for non-technical pet owners, I developed LLM-based pipelines that translate natural language commands directly into robot behaviors. A user saying "go find the dog" triggers a full localization + navigation goal without any app navigation.

When localization fails (common in real homes with changing layouts), smart re-localization pipelines automatically recover the robot's position using visual landmarks.

## Object & Human Following

I implemented bespoke following behavior by integrating vision-based detection (camera + depth) with a custom **DWB (Dynamic Window Approach)** controller plugin. This allows ORo to follow the dog or a person smoothly, maintaining safe distance and responding to sudden direction changes.

## Full-Stack Connectivity

The mobile app communicates with the robot through a **FastAPI-based backend** that bridges ROS 2 topics and services with cloud infrastructure. This architecture enabled:

- Real-time status updates pushed to the app via WebSockets
- Remote task assignment (e.g., schedule treat time from phone)
- OTA firmware and software updates via CI/CD pipelines

## Deployment & OTA

One of the biggest operational challenges was deploying software updates to a fleet of home robots. I championed and implemented a **CI/CD pipeline with OTA (Over-The-Air) update support**, ensuring that:

- Embedded, perception, and control software stacks could be updated remotely
- Rollbacks were automated on failure detection
- Fleet-wide deployments were staged and monitored

## Outcomes

- Delivered multiple shippable robot iterations with cross-functional team of 8 across 2+ years
- Built a fully autonomous indoor navigation system with ROS 2 + Nav2 performing reliably in real home environments
- Integrated LLM-based natural language control, making the robot accessible to non-technical users
- Established OTA update infrastructure enabling continuous product improvement post-deployment
